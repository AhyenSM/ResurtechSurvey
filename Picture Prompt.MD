# Resurtech Survey - Picture Prompts

Use these prompts in Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Stable Diffusion to generate a hero image for the first page of the Resurtech survey. They are designed to match the specific "dark mode with neon/emerald green accents" aesthetic of the application.

### Option 1: The AI/CAD Morph (Recommended)
> **Prompt:** A hyper-realistic, sleek 3D printing extruder head actively printing a complex, futuristic geometric shape. The background is a dark, moody charcoal void. Glowing neon green (#39ff14) laser lines and emerald green wireframe meshes surround the object, symbolizing AI-assisted CAD design morphing into a physical object. The lighting is cinematic and dramatic, with soft frosted glassmorphism elements floating in the background. High-tech, premium, and modern aesthetic. 8k, Unreal Engine 5 render style.

### Option 2: The Designer's Desk
> **Prompt:** A modern industrial designer's workspace in a dimly lit room with dark aesthetic styling. A glowing computer monitor displays a complex wireframe 3D model in emerald and neon green. Next to the monitor is a sleek, black, futuristic 3D printer bathed in a soft neon green rim light. Dark, premium tech vibe with deep shadows and vibrant green accents. Depth of field, macro photography, cinematic lighting.

### Option 3: Abstract Digital Fabrication
> **Prompt:** Abstract 3D rendering of digital fabrication. A dark void background with intersecting translucent frosted glass panels. Vibrant neon green and emerald green data streams and glowing CAD grid lines forming the shape of a mechanical gear or robotic part. Premium, sleek, futuristic technology concept, minimalist but highly detailed, glowing accents.

---

### Tips for Best Results:
*   **Colors to enforce:** "Deep charcoal", "dark void", "emerald green", "neon green".
*   **Keywords to keep:** "Glassmorphism", "sleek", "premium", "3D printing", "wireframe".
*   **Aspect Ratio:** If generating for a hero banner on the first page, use a wide aspect ratio like 16:9 (`--ar 16:9` in Midjourney). If generating for a side image, a 4:5 or 1:1 ratio might work better.
